# Changelog

## 0.0.1 — first public release

- Published as **tidyfigs**.
- Three modes: image → editable draw.io, text → three figure prototypes, enhance for publication.
- Readable sources in `src/`; the single-file `drawio_toolkit.py` bundle is generated by
  `tools/build_bundle.py` and checked in CI.
- Six text-mode and four image-mode worked examples, themes (navy default, Okabe–Ito, grayscale)
  and venue presets (IEEE, Elsevier, Springer LNCS, thesis, slides).
